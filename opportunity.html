How Does This Work?

Commission, Built Into Every Booking

Every time a holiday is booked, commission is already built into the price. As an Independent Travel Agent, that commission comes back to you — book for yourself, friends, family, or your own customers, and earn around the life you already have.

So You Can Earn

Three Ways To Benefit

On Your Travel

Make more memories by using it to stretch your own holiday budget — earning commission back on your own bookings and enjoying industry insider benefits.

From Your Travel

Choose to earn a flexible, uncapped income that fits around what you already do — booking travel for others, as a side hustle or a career.

By Sharing

Choose to recommend the opportunity to others and build a passive, residual income helping them do the same as an independent agent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Do I need travel experience?

No travel experience is needed. You'll get full training and step-by-step support from day one.

Can I do this alongside my current job?

Yes — most agents build this around an existing job, family life, or both, starting small and growing at their own pace.

Can I use this for my own holidays?

Absolutely — plenty of people join purely to book their own travel and earn commission back on it.

Is there any training?

Yes, full training and ongoing mentoring are included from day one, with support whenever you need it.

Do I have to work set hours?

No — you set your own hours and grow the business at whatever pace suits your life.
